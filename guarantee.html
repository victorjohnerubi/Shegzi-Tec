Our 100% Certainty Guarantee

Your logo deserves to be right.

We’ll agree the artwork, production details and expected dispatch date with you before your custom order moves into production.

The Shegzi-Tech Certainty Promise

We aim to print your order to the proof you approve and keep to the dispatch date confirmed in your quote. If something goes wrong, contact our team so we can review it and make things right.

  • Print accuracy: We’ll compare the finished design with the final proof you approved.
  • On-time delivery: We’ll confirm a dispatch date before production and let you know if circumstances affect it.

How returns and reprints work

Because custom products are made with your unique artwork, a change of mind after proof approval may not be eligible for a return. If items arrive damaged, defective, misprinted or differ from the approved proof, contact us promptly with your order details and photos so we can assess the issue.

Report an issue

  1. Inspect your order as soon as it arrives.
  2. Share your order number and clear photos with us by phone or WhatsApp.
  3. We’ll review the issue with you and discuss the next steps.
Contact Us on WhatsApp

Order changes and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. Before proof approval, changes are generally easier to accommodate. Once you approve your proof and production begins, changes or cancellations may not be possible. We’ll confirm the options for your specific order.

The specific terms, delivery dates and resolution options for your order will be confirmed in your quote and order communications.
