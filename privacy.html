Privacy Policy

This page explains how Shegzi-Tech may handle information you share when you contact us or request design and printing services.

1. Information you share

When you contact us or request a quote, you may share your name, company name, email address, phone number, billing or delivery details, order specifications and artwork files. If you use a third-party service such as WhatsApp, that service handles information according to its own privacy terms.

2. How information may be used

We use project details to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, review artwork, coordinate production and delivery, and provide customer support. We may also use technical information made available by your browser to maintain and improve the website.

3. Artwork and intellectual property

Your submitted artwork remains yours. We use files you provide to assess and fulfil your project. We will not intentionally display your proprietary designs in a public showcase without your permission.

4. Sharing and storage

We do not sell your personal information. To respond to or fulfil a request, we may need to share relevant details with service providers involved in production, communication, payment or delivery. Please do not send payment card details through website forms or WhatsApp.

5. Your choices and requests

You can contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information you have shared, subject to any records we must keep. Call or WhatsApp +234 816 414 2389 and include enough detail for us to identify your request.

This policy describes the current contact and quote-request website. It should be reviewed and updated if online accounts, payments, analytics, advertising or order tracking are introduced.
