Our Services

Complete Design, Tech & Printing Solutions Under One Roof

At Shegzi-Tech, we eliminate the stress of working with multiple vendors. Whether you are launching a new brand, refreshing your digital presence or printing high-impact marketing materials, we combine modern technology with precision craftsmanship to deliver results that stand out.

01

Graphic & Brand Identity Design

Great printing starts with great design. Our creative team uses industry-standard design tools to build visual identities for screen and print.

  • Logo & Brand Identity: Custom logos, colour palettes, typography and brand style guides.
  • Marketing & Promotional Graphics: Flyers, brochures, posters and social media visual kits.
  • UI/UX & Digital Assets: Web graphics, pitch decks and digital banners for tech-forward businesses.
02

Commercial & Corporate Print

Make a lasting impression in every meeting and transaction with crisp, professionally finished business essentials.

  • Business Cards: Standard, matte, glossy, spot UV and textured finishes.
  • Office Stationery: Custom letterheads, branded envelopes, presentation folders and receipt booklets.
  • Publications & Catalogs: Company profiles, annual reports, magazines and product catalogs.
03

Large-Format Printing & Signage

Command attention indoors and outdoors with vibrant prints designed for visibility at events and in your space.

  • Event & Display Stands: Roll-up banners, X-banners, teardrop flags and media backdrops.
  • Outdoor & Indoor Signage: Flex banners, self-adhesive vinyl, window graphics and directional signs.
  • Wall Art & Posters: Canvas prints, mounted boards and large promotional posters.
04

Custom Packaging & Product Labels

Turn your products into shelf-stoppers with custom packaging that protects your goods and elevates your brand.

  • Product Boxes: Custom retail boxes, mailer boxes and gift packaging.
  • Labels & Stickers: Product labels, seals and custom-shaped stickers.
  • Branded Bags: Custom paper carrier bags, nylon bags and reusable totes.
05

Branded Merchandise & Corporate Souvenirs

Keep your brand top-of-mind with custom-printed apparel and promotional items for events, staff and clients.

  • Custom Apparel: Printed or embroidered T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies and caps.
  • Executive Gifts: Branded notebooks, pens, insulated flasks, ceramic mugs and flash drives.
  • Event Souvenirs: Custom lanyards, ID cards, wristbands and award plaques.
