Terms of Service

Clear expectations for every project

These general terms apply when you request or place an order for Shegzi-Tech design and printing services. Your quote and order confirmation provide the specific details for your project.

1. Quotes and orders

Quotes are based on the product, quantity, artwork, finishing and delivery information available at the time. We’ll confirm applicable pricing, payment arrangements, estimated timelines and delivery details with you before production begins.

2. Artwork and proof approval

You are responsible for reviewing your proof, including spelling, colours, sizing, placement and other supplied details. Production starts after your approval. Changes requested after approval may affect cost and timing, and may not be possible once production has started.

3. Production and delivery

Production and delivery estimates depend on the product, quantity, artwork approval and destination. The specific schedule agreed in your quote or order confirmation applies to your project. We’ll contact you if a material change to the schedule arises.

4. Custom orders and concerns

Custom-made goods are produced to your approved specifications. If an order arrives damaged, defective or materially different from its approved proof, contact us promptly with the order details and photos so we can review the issue. See our Certainty Guarantee & Returns for further information.

5. Questions and changes

Contact us before approving your proof if you need to change your order. We’ll confirm available options and any effect on price or timing. Questions about these terms can be sent by phone or WhatsApp at +234 816 414 2389.

Your confirmed quote and order communications take precedence for the specific product, price, production schedule and delivery arrangements agreed for your order.
