Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our design and print services, preparing artwork, quotes and delivery.

General & Services

1. What services does Shegzi-Tech offer?

We offer digital design and physical printing solutions, including logo and brand identity design, graphic design, business cards, flyers, brochures, banners, signage, posters, custom packaging and branded corporate merchandise.

2. Do I need my own design before placing a print order?

No. If you have a print-ready design, we can review it. If you’re starting from scratch, our design team can work with you to create custom artwork.

3. Can you help rebrand or update an existing logo?

Yes. We can help modernize a logo, refine brand colours and create a consistent visual identity for digital and print materials.

Artwork & File Preparation

4. What file formats do you accept for printing?

For sharp print quality, we prefer high-resolution PDF, AI, EPS or PSD files. We also accept high-resolution PNG and JPEG files. For raster artwork, 300 DPI at the intended print size is recommended. See our Artwork & Print Guidelines for more tips.

5. Will I see a proof before you print my order?

Yes. We share a digital proof for you to review the layout, spelling and colours before production. A physical test print may also be available for bulk or specialized orders on request.

6. Why can printed colours look different from my screen?

Screens display colour using light (RGB), while printers use ink (CMYK). We prepare print artwork with colour reproduction in mind, though exact matching can vary between screens, materials and print methods.

Ordering, Pricing & Delivery

7. What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard print jobs such as business cards, flyers and posters typically take 2–4 business days after design approval. Larger projects, custom packaging and bulk branding orders typically take 5–7 business days. Timing depends on the project; ask us about express same-day or next-day options when requesting a quote.

8. Is there a minimum order quantity?

It depends on the item and print method. Digital printing may suit smaller batches, while offset printing and custom merchandise can require higher minimums. Tell us the item and quantity you need for guidance.

9. How do I get a quote?

Send us the item, size, quantity, deadline and whether you need design assistance using the contact form or WhatsApp. We’ll review your requirements and provide a project-specific quote.

10. Do you offer delivery?

You can arrange collection from our print hub or ask us to dispatch your finished order. Share your delivery location and we’ll confirm available options and timing.
