Who We Are

Innovative technology meets creative design.

Welcome to Shegzi-Tech, a technology, creative design and precision printing company headquartered in Ogun State, Nigeria. Founded by John Oluwasegun, we bridge digital design and physical production so businesses, schools and brands can bring their ideas to life under one roof.

From custom corporate apparel and executive souvenirs to event banners, product packaging and commercial prints, we combine modern design technology with precision printing. We serve growing businesses in Abeokuta and Ota, as well as clients across Nigeria.
